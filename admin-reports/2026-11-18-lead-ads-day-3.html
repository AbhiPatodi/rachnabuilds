What we're seeing

  • The new videos keep beating the old ad. Videos 1 and 2 brought 5 of the 6 leads at ₹543 and ₹562 each. The old image ad has spent ₹3,238 (54% of the budget) for 1 lead. That lead did book the first call, so it isn't worthless, but per lead it costs about 6 times more.
  • Meta keeps pushing budget to the old ad. It has the cheapest CPM (₹7,069), so the algorithm favours it even though it converts worst (8% of clicks become leads vs 23–33% on the videos).
  • The US number solved reachability. 3 leads aren't on WhatsApp, and texts from the Indian number didn't deliver. Since 17 Nov they've had a text from a US number plus an email. No reply yet, which is normal within 24 hours.
  • Lead quality is still mixed. 5 of 6 say under $5k a month on ads and under 1% conversion. 3 of 6 typed something other than a store link. One lead (Harmat) is an agency, which may be a partner opportunity rather than a store.
  • Every lead goes to a call. We don't send free written reports. Leads are offered a short call where Rachna shows what she found on screen.

Next steps

  • Today: prepare the Tallow & Vine store audit to show on tonight's call (11:00 PM IST). Contact Harmat by text and email and ask for a call time.
  • 19 Nov: follow up with Sarah, Chasity and Jemelia. Top up the ad balance (about ₹7,900 left, roughly two days at the current pace).
  • 21–23 Nov: after 5–7 days of learning, decide on the old image ad. If it still costs over ₹2,000 per lead, turn it off and let the two videos take the full budget.
  • Next fresh campaign (planned, not live): broad audience instead of stacked interests, a higher-intent form, and a question asking how they prefer to be contacted (WhatsApp, text, call or email).
