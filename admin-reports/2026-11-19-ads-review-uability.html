8 · What the data says

  • The creative problem is solved; the structure problem is not. Both new videos sit above 5% CTR with ₹700-ish leads. But they share one ad set with the old static ad, which still takes more than half the budget at ₹2,700 per lead. Meta is pacing by its own history, not by what works now.
  • Targeting is still the August setup. Five states, three interests, Advantage Audience off, Facebook placements on — the exact settings the 11 Nov audit failed and the 12 Nov plan replaced. CPM is ₹7,600–8,700 (~$85–100). That is in the range UAbility calls normal for US leads, so the lever now is lead quality, not CPM.
  • Lead quality is the real cost. 6 of 7 leads are sub-$5k, sub-1% stores; two have no store yet; three can't be reached on WhatsApp. The form has no budget-qualifying question (the 12 Nov plan had "projects start at $1,500 — fits your budget?").
  • Speed to contact is fixed. Every lead got a personal message within hours (median under 2 h for the last five), a site read before the message, and a call ask instead of a free report. One in seven reached a call in under 48 h.
  • Money: ₹36,192 media spend lifetime plus GST, ₹6,540 banked, one proposal open. The restart's break-even is a single $500 sale.
